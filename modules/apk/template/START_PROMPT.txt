Analyze the authorized APK configured in `target/TARGET.toml`.

If usable preparation output already exists under `extracted/jadx/`, `extracted/apktool/` and `reports/tool-output/` for the current APK, reuse it instead of repeating expensive decompilation. Otherwise run `tools/apk_prepare.py`.

Read `[orchestration]` from TARGET.toml. Respect `max_parallel_agents` for concurrently executing delegated tasks, defaulting to 2 when absent.

Map the attack surface first, then perform prioritized static security analysis. Keep the primary context small. After preparation, triage the deterministic hard-coded secret/credential candidates in `reports/tool-output/secret-candidates.*` with `apk-secret-hunter`; do not treat pattern hits as findings until local usage and classification are established.

For public research use local-first behavior: first determine whether existing Java/Smali/XML/resources, metadata, hashes, certificate parsing, or a focused local subagent can answer the uncertainty. Only externally answerable residual questions go to `apk-researcher`. Respect `research_max_questions`, `research_max_sources_per_question`, and `research_max_report_words` from TARGET.toml, defaulting to 3, 5 and 900.

`findings/research.md` is only a compact index. Each public question gets exactly one canonical detail file under `reports/research/RQ-XX-....md`; do not create a separate batch-summary report. Perform cheap deterministic local correlation after research before marking something unresolved. Search-only source leads must not change findings without verified source support and local applicability.

Maintain the structured workspace records throughout the run:
- `findings/inventory.md`
- `findings/attack-surface.md`
- `findings/secrets.md`
- `findings/findings.md`
- `findings/coverage.md`
- `findings/research.md`
- `findings/analysis-log.md`

Detailed non-research work belongs in `reports/subagents/`; one canonical detail artifact per research question belongs in `reports/research/`.

The final report must not replace the structured `findings/` records.
