Analyze the authorized APK configured in `target/TARGET.toml`.

If usable preparation output already exists under `extracted/jadx/`, `extracted/apktool/` and `reports/tool-output/` for the current APK, reuse it instead of repeating expensive decompilation. Otherwise run `tools/apk_prepare.py`.

Read `[orchestration]` from TARGET.toml. Respect `max_parallel_agents` for concurrently executing delegated tasks, defaulting to 2 when absent.

Map the attack surface first, then perform prioritized static security analysis. Keep the primary context small.

Use targeted public research when a concrete external fact could materially change severity, applicability or the next analysis step. Respect `research_max_questions` and `research_max_sources_per_question` from TARGET.toml, defaulting to 3 and 5. First record the narrow question and local evidence in `findings/research.md`, then delegate the bounded batch to `apk-researcher`. Detailed source notes belong under `reports/research/`. Public research never replaces local validation, and important research-backed conclusions should be independently validated.

Maintain the structured workspace records throughout the run:
- `findings/inventory.md`
- `findings/attack-surface.md`
- `findings/secrets.md`
- `findings/findings.md`
- `findings/coverage.md`
- `findings/research.md`
- `findings/analysis-log.md`

`findings/coverage.md` must state which installed analysis tools were actually used or intentionally skipped and why. `findings/analysis-log.md` must record delegated subagent tasks and their result-file paths.

Detailed delegated work belongs in `reports/subagents/`; detailed public research belongs in `reports/research/`.
At completion create `reports/STATIC_SECURITY_REPORT.md` as the consolidated human-readable report, including limitations, validation status, relevant research context and a short Tools/Coverage summary. The final report must not replace the structured `findings/` records.
